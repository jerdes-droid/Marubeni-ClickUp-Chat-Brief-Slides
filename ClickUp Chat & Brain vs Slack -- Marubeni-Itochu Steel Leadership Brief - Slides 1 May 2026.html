Marubeni-Itochu Steelvii. Anticipated questions
From the room

The four questions you will be asked, and the honest answers.

Is ClickUp Chat as mature as Slack?
For the messaging primitives the group uses every day, yes. Slack has more consumer-app integrations; ClickUp wins on the integration that matters: your deals, quotes, and AI agents.
Why not just buy Slack and keep ClickUp for 30?
Because that locks fragmentation in. The 30 build the data, the 270 talk about it in a tool that cannot see it. Brain closes the gap for less than Slack.
Are we paying twice for AI?
No. Brain bundles ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Premium Reasoning into a single per-seat fee. Most groups consolidate shadow-IT AI rather than add to it.
Compliance and data residency?
Same controls already approved: SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA. Brain does not train foundation models on customer data. Nothing new for IT to evaluate.
